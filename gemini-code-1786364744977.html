CSE Success Plays Menu

The Customer Success Engineer (CSE) role supports Sales, Renewal Managers, and CSMs in executing predefined plays[cite: 1].

  • Accelerate Plays
  • Respond Plays
  • Internal Plays
  • Exclusions
  • Engage & Resources

Accelerate Plays

Designed to accelerate time-to-value, customer adoption, and overall maturity[cite: 1].

Identity Security Program Guidance ACTIVE

Provide prescriptive guidance on Identity Security program based on the Blueprint framework[cite: 1].

Description: Customers looking for prescriptive guidance on their overall Identity Security program based on the CyberArk Blueprint framework[cite: 1]. Accelerates time-to-value and achieves business outcomes, especially for customers who recently finished their initial project or want a long-term strategy[cite: 1].

Qualifications

  • Customer received high-level overview of Blueprint and self-service materials[cite: 1].
  • Intended audience and meeting goals established[cite: 1].
  • Aware that building a final roadmap is out of scope for this engagement[cite: 1].

Expected Outcomes

  • Customized mid-level Blueprint presentation delivered[cite: 1].
  • Customer receives presentation and materials to build a custom roadmap[cite: 1].
  • Services follow up if further roadmap assistance is needed[cite: 1].

Identity Security Use Case Review ACTIVE

Provide use case-specific best practice recommendations[cite: 1].

Description: Guidance or considerations on specific use cases fitting within the Blueprint framework[cite: 1]. Effective early in the CyberArk journey (e.g., post-jumpstart/implementation) for designing/planning specific use cases[cite: 1].

Qualifications

  • Goals and appropriate audience secured[cite: 1].
  • Customer aware that implementation is not in scope[cite: 1].
  • Not for active struggles/ongoing issues (use Intervention play)[cite: 1].

Expected Outcomes

  • Shared Success team member delivers a "show and tell" best-practice meeting[cite: 1].
  • Direct walkthrough or validation provided as needed[cite: 1].
  • Services required for actual implementation[cite: 1].

Privileged Access Discovery Guidance ACTIVE

Show-and-tell walkthrough of tools and processes to discover privilege[cite: 1].

Description: Helps customers discover and assess privileged access across Windows/UNIX Servers, Workstations, or Cloud Service Providers[cite: 1]. Drives adoption by identifying new use cases and creating urgency to remediate vulnerabilities[cite: 1].

Qualifications

  • Target privilege scope explicitly defined (pick one target scenario)[cite: 1].
  • Customer aware execution/analysis is out of scope[cite: 1].
  • Customer must work with Sales to obtain DNA license or CEM trial tenant[cite: 1].

Expected Outcomes

  • Guided walkthrough on installing, configuring, and setting up discovery tools[cite: 1].
  • Explanation of how to review DNA or CEM outputs using examples[cite: 1].
  • Materials and training provided for self-service assessments[cite: 1].

Respond Plays

Designed to respond to stagnant or lagging customer adoption[cite: 1].

Product-Specific Intervention ACTIVE

Resolve customer roadblock or challenges impacting adoption[cite: 1].

Description: Provides tactical assistance reactively to overcome adoption blockers[cite: 1]. Reduces churn and unlocks adoption opportunities, particularly for technical or political struggles with a specific use case[cite: 1].

Qualifications

  • Roadblock is NOT related to a missing product feature or enhancement[cite: 1].
  • Customer aware implementation is out of scope[cite: 1].
  • Not for new use case onboarding (use Use Case Review instead)[cite: 1].

Expected Outcomes

  • Meeting held to address specific blockers with tactical best practices[cite: 1].
  • Customer educated on self-help routes (Community, Support)[cite: 1].
  • Services required for implementation assistance[cite: 1].

Identity Security Utilization Insight ACTIVE

Deliver insights about adoption, consumption, and utilization[cite: 1].

Description: Helps customers understand CyberArk PAM implementation KPIs using Telemetry data[cite: 1]. Drives alignment on goals and time-to-value[cite: 1].

Qualifications

  • For Privilege Cloud or Self-Hosted PAM customers with telemetry[cite: 1].
  • Must have telemetry installed or agree to install it[cite: 1].

Expected Outcomes

  • Meeting to review Telemetry output and recommend next steps[cite: 1].
  • Option to assist Self-Hosted PAM customers with a 30-min setup call[cite: 1].
  • Includes Vault Health Check Assessment Tool (VAT) checkups[cite: 1].
Vault Health Check Assessment Tool (VAT): Covered under the Identity Security Utilization Insight play to ensure successful implementations and maximize product utilization[cite: 1].

Internal Plays

Supporting internal CyberArk Account Team activities[cite: 1].

Tactical Account Planning Assistance ACTIVE

Provide AE insight about Customer current state for account plan activities[cite: 1].

Description: Tactical guidance for the CyberArk Account Team on interpreting customer state and potential next steps during account planning (renewals, business reviews, upsells)[cite: 1].

Qualifications

  • For existing Privilege Cloud or Self-Hosted PAM with telemetry[cite: 1].
  • Internal meeting ONLY (not customer-facing)[cite: 1].

Expected Outcomes

  • Internal review of adoption, compliance, and coverage data[cite: 1].
  • Recommendations on next steps for account strategy[cite: 1].

Service Exclusions

The CSE engagement scope explicitly excludes the following activities[cite: 1]:

  • Software selling: Engage Sales or Solution Engineers when opportunities are identified[cite: 1].
  • Implementation activities: Follow-up items are routed to Professional Services (PS)[cite: 1].
  • Jump starts[cite: 1]
  • Weekend Standby: Migration or upgrade standby support over weekends[cite: 1].
  • MIM / Venafi health checks[cite: 1]
  • Full-blown PAM health checks: VAT checkups are covered, but full PAM health checks are excluded[cite: 1].
