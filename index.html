Accelerate Plays

Accelerate time-to-value, customer adoption, and maturity.

Identity Security Program Guidance

Active
Provide prescriptive guidance on Identity Security program based on the Blueprint framework.

Helps accelerate customer time-to-value, drive adoption, and achieve business outcomes. Highly effective for customers finishing initial projects or establishing long-term strategies.

Qualifications

  • Customer has received Blueprint overview and knows self-service materials.
  • Intended audience, meeting goals, and key stakeholders secured.
  • Customer understands custom roadmap building is out of scope.

Expected Outcomes

  • Mid-level customized Blueprint presentation provided.
  • Customer receives presentation and support materials (Success Blog, Toolkit).
  • Services follow-up recommended for full roadmap assistance.

Identity Security Use Case Review

Active
Provide use case-specific best practice recommendations.

Assistance with designing or planning specific use cases (e.g., local database accounts, ISV integration, UNIX root accounts) early in the Idira journey.

Qualifications

  • Intended audience and goals established and committed.
  • Customer aware implementation is out of scope.
  • Not for active technical struggles (use Product-Specific Intervention).

Expected Outcomes

  • Discussion and best practice walkthrough based on Blueprint.
  • Optional pre-conversation to gather context if needed.
  • Show-and-tell or direct validation exercise.

Privileged Access Discovery Guidance

Active
Show-and-tell walkthrough of tools and processes to discover privilege.

Guides customers through tools/processes for Windows/UNIX Servers, Workstations, and Cloud Service Providers to identify privilege vulnerabilities and drive adoption.

Qualifications

  • Defined scope (Windows, UNIX, Workstations, or Cloud).
  • Target audience committed and goals set.
  • Execution/analysis out of scope; DNA license or CEM trial tenant required via Sales.

Expected Outcomes

  • Guided meeting on tools/resources for privilege discovery.
  • Installation, configuration best practices, and output walkthrough (Discovery SaaS/CEM).
  • Customer receives materials, documentation, and training guides.

Respond Plays

Respond to stagnant or lagging customer adoption.

Product-Specific Intervention

Active
Resolve customer roadblocks or challenges impacting adoption.

Tactical assistance to overcome technical or political blockers with specific use cases, reducing churn and unlocking new adoption opportunities.

Qualifications

  • Roadblock is not related to missing product features/enhancements.
  • Audience committed; customer aware implementation is out of scope.
  • Not intended for getting started with brand new use cases.

Expected Outcomes

  • Targeted guidance to resolve technical/political blockers.
  • Education on support channels (Community, Support).
  • Services recommendation if direct implementation is needed.

Identity Security Utilization Insight

Active
Deliver insights about adoption, consumption, and utilization.

Helps customer understand common KPIs of their Idira PAM implementation using telemetry data, evaluating overall value and planning future goals.

Qualifications

  • Existing Privilege Cloud or Self-Hosted PAM customers with telemetry.
  • Must have or be willing to install telemetry (default on Privilege Cloud).
  • Key stakeholders identified and committed.

Expected Outcomes

  • Review Telemetry output & recommendations on next use cases.
  • Vault Health Check Assessment Tool (VAT) integration if applicable.
  • Guidance on self-service resources (Community, Docs, Success Blogs).

Internal Plays

Supporting internal Idira Account Team activities.

Tactical Account Planning Assistance

Active
Provide AE insight about Customer current state for account plan activities.

Tactical guidance for interpreting customer usage and current state to inform sales strategies, renewals, business reviews, or upsell opportunities.

Qualifications

  • For existing Privilege Cloud or Self-Hosted PAM with telemetry.
  • Internal-only call with Account Team (Not customer-facing).

Expected Outcomes

  • Internal meeting to review adoption, compliance, and coverage metrics.
  • Actionable recommendations on next steps for the account strategy.

Service Exclusions

Activities out of scope for Customer Success Engineers.

Software selling (Engage Sales/SEs)
Implementation activities (PS follow-up)
Jump starts
Migration/upgrade standby over weekends
MIM / Venafi health checks
Full blown PAM health check (VAT allowed)
